The sourcing flow

Zapeks works as a practical link between customer demand and supplier availability.

  1. Customer RFQ

    The customer shares the requirement, quantity, timing, target conditions, and approved alternates.

  2. Sourcing review

    Zapeks searches viable supplier channels and evaluates availability against the specification.

  3. Response and validation

    Options are reviewed for technical fit, cost, quality, documentation, and timing.

  4. Logistics arrangement

    Shipment, warehousing, consolidation, and the delivery route are coordinated.

  5. Delivery

    Parts reach the customer with the agreed handoff information.

What helps an RFQ move quickly

Good input reduces quote cycles and makes supplier options easier to compare.

Part identification

Part number, description, and approved alternates.

Quantity and timing

Required quantity, target date, and delivery location.

Quality requirements

Compliance, traceability, and documentation expectations.

Supplier preferences

Preferred suppliers or sources to avoid.

Commercial targets

Price goals and the criteria for choosing an option.

Delivery constraints

Warehousing, logistics, and handoff requirements.
